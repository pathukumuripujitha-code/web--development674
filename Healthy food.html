Popular Healthy Recipes

Fresh Veggie Salad

🥗 180 calories | 10 minutes

A refreshing salad rich in vitamins and fiber.

Ingredients:
  • Lettuce and spinach
  • Cucumber and tomato
  • Carrot and lemon juice
  • Olive oil and pepper

Fruit & Oats Bowl

🥣 300 calories | 10 minutes

A filling breakfast with fiber and natural sweetness.

Ingredients:
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ banana
  • Apple and nuts

Vegetable Soup

🍲 150 calories | 25 minutes

A warm, light dish packed with vegetables.

Ingredients:
  • Carrot and beans
  • Chopped tomato
  • Onion and garlic
  • Water and herbs

Brown Rice Bowl

🍚 350 calories | 30 minutes

A balanced meal with whole grains and vegetables.

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Cooked chickpeas
  • Olive oil and spices
